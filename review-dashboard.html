Welcome to the Review Dashboard

This dashboard helps you review automated speed-limit predictions for roadway segment endpoints. You can inspect heuristic and visual analysis results, view the location on a map, and record your agreement or corrections.

Use Table View for an overview of all cases, then switch to Case View when you want detailed review controls for one case at a time.
